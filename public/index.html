How it works

Three steps, and the object remembers all of them.

  1. 1

    Pin it

    List something you no longer need. Search for your place, or click the map, to drop a pin where it has lived.

  2. 2

    Tell its story

    Write a few lines about what it has been through. A short, true story makes a used thing worth choosing over a new one.

  3. 3

    Pass it on

    When it finds a new home, you enter the new owner's email. They add their own chapter, and its journey grows on the map, one neighborhood at a time.

Cebu already knows the value of used things

From the stalls of Carbon Market to the ukay-ukay racks near Colon, secondhand shopping is part of daily life here. Hand-me-downs and repairs are normal, and a good find is worth talking about.

Secondhand Atlas adds the part that is usually lost: who had it before you, and what it has done. We think that history is what makes an object worth keeping in circulation.

  • Local first

    Objects are listed by neighborhood, so pickups happen close to home.

  • Story as proof

    Every item shows its full chain of owners, so you can see where it really comes from.

  • Free items welcome

    Give something away for ₱0 and still pass its story on.
